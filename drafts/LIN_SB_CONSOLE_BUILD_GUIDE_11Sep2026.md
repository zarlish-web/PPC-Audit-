## Check these three first

Without all three, the campaign will not save.

1. **Brand registry** active on the account
2. **Brand logo** in the Creative Asset Library — 400×400px minimum, white or transparent background
3. **Brand name** — the account uses both `DECOLURE` and `Decolure`. Pick whichever the registry shows and keep it the same across all six

**Landing page.** Sponsored Brands cannot land on a product detail page. With no linen Store, it will land on Amazon's generated collection page — expected, not a mistake. If a Store exists, use it.

## Why only these SKUs

Every SKU here is **FUND tier** — the 12 refunding 0–16%. The BLOCK tier (37.5–45.5% refunds) and FLOOR tier (25–30%) are deliberately excluded: their campaigns were just paused or budget-bounded in the SP deployment, and putting them on a new surface would undo that.

## A caveat on the bids

**$0.53 is inherited from the SP build, not derived from SB economics.** Sponsored Brands buys a different placement at a different price. It is a reasonable opening number and nothing more — read it properly at the 23 September check rather than treating it as settled.
