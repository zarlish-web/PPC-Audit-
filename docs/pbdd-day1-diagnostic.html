Each deal product: why, and what to do today

US Bamboo 4 Piece · PED · Exceeding

What happened
373 units vs 290 plan (×1.29), profit $2,348 vs $1,226 plan. Ads $2,111 vs $1,042 plan; TACoS 9.2% vs the 6% cap (break-even 17.8%). Twin push earned (bamboo sheets twin 50 → 18). Cooling push lost (cooling bamboo sheets 12 → 43).
Root cause
The PED tripled the enrolled children (6.6× normal) and 71% of units were organic. The overspend sat in two boosted autos: Full Grey at $3.75 CPC (normal $0.58, 53% ACoS) and King White at $4.73 (normal $0.80). The Cooling exact paid $6.51 a click at +288% TOS and was dark from 05:07 PT, so the Cooling terms had no ad support for most of the day while rivals ran deals on them.
Day 2
Keep branded defence, the own-ASIN PAT and the "bamboo sheets queen" exact funded all day (Day-1 run-out math puts branded broad at about $600, not the $200–300 now). Cap the re-enabled Cooling exact at TOS ≤ +100%, or pause the Cooling push and read it after the event. Keep Queen White and Queen Creme off ads: about 2 days of cover at deal pace, with inbound not yet live.

US Bamboo 6 Piece · PED · Missing on profit

What happened
390 units vs 379 plan (×1.03), but profit $2,311 vs $2,719 plan (−$408). Ads $1,344 vs $557 plan (2.4×); TACoS 5.3% vs the 2.3% cap. All five King terms and viscose bamboo sheets gained 1–10 places.
Root cause
The plan's 2.3% cap couldn't fund a rank push and a profit floor at once. Boosts wasted the push money: exact "bamboo sheets king" paid $8.63 a click for 0 orders before 05:09 PT, and the Queen White 6pc auto paid $4.65 for 1 order. King rank rose on deal sales velocity, not on the King exacts, which were dark from 05:09 PT.
Day 2
Keep the King pushes at normal top-of-search (test A) with budgets sized to last to 21:00 PT. Keep the aged-SKU clearance auto (18% ACoS) funded through the evening. Queen Navy 6pc has about 1 day of cover at deal pace and 81% of its allocation used, so keep its ads off. Check whether the B6 PAT on our own B4 hero (B08KQHRDDG) is just moving sales between our own listings.

US Satin 4 Piece · LD → PED · Missing

What happened
294 units vs 356 plan (×0.83). Ads $902 vs $358 plan; TACoS 10.9% over the 10.6% break-even. Profit $373 is $97 above a normal day. The LD sold 280 of 1,037 committed units (27%). "satin sheets" held (#3 → #2); satin sheets queen 6 → 9 and silk sheets queen 7 → 10.

Satin doesn't respond much to price (about 1.4× lift vs 3.1× on bamboo). The LD started about 07:45 PT with a promotion record that still read CANCELED, and its hero, Queen Black at $23.75, earned about $0.94 a unit before ads. White Queen exact "satin sheets queen" took $183 at 53% ACoS.

Decide the PED this morning: it is live on 65 SKUs, pace is 0.56× and TACoS is 13.4%. If it can't be pulled, stop deal-price ad money on Queen Black, hold the M63 satin-Black ad adds until Oct 8, and defend the two queen terms at full price on in-stock non-hero colours. Re-read at 11:00 PT against the 10.6% break-even.

Account level: is the strategy delivering?

Against the business goals

  • Profit (primary goal): met. $9.6K vs a $5.0K plan and a $2.5K normal day on the board's basis. US alone +18% vs plan. Sellerboard confirms US profit ($10,410 before reserve vs the board's $10,562) but puts Canada at about half the board's figure.
  • Rank (push objective): mixed. Gained on B6 King (+6 to +11), viscose bamboo sheets (+10) and B4 Twin (+10, +32). Lost on B4 Cooling (−9 to −31) and the satin queen terms (−3). Best-seller rank in Sheet & Pillowcase Sets barely moved (B4 150 → 145, B6 138 → 159): competitors' deals lifted the category as much as ours lifted us. No share was gained.
  • Efficiency: missed. Ad spend 2.1× plan. US ad ACoS 32% against break-evens of 10.6–17.8%, so ads lost money on the orders they were credited with. The day paid only because about 75% of units carried no ad cost.
  • Volume: met. 1.21× plan, 3.3× a normal day. Push products were on or ahead of plan except Satin.
  • Stock: protected. No hero ran short. Canada's committed deal units were the limit, not FBA stock.

Root causes, ranked by money

  • 1. Canada was planned at a quarter of its demand. It is +$3.7K on the board's numbers, plus deal units lost on 15 B4 children. A planning gap, not a PPC one, and the Day-2 forecast repeats it.
  • 2. Budget sizing. Converters (branded, own-ASIN, clearance autos, Queen exacts) ran dry before noon; pushes were floored at $5. About $6.3K of ad sales near break-even went unbought, roughly $1–1.5K of profit.
  • 3. Placement multipliers. TOS +200–900% and ROS up to +692% on 9 autos/exacts: about $740 of CPC premium on Oct 6, plus the Oct 5 pre-event spend (section 06). No conversion gain.
  • 4. Products with no deal leaked. $1,768 at 17% TACoS. Rest-of-search and product-page clicks ran 53–69% ACoS. Five unplanned deals paid $100 fees and lost about $350 against a normal day.
  • 5. Satin deal economics. A weak price response (about 1.4×) on a hero that earns about $0 at the deal price. The LD sold 27% of its allocation, and today's PED repeats it.
  • 6. The plan's caps. TACoS caps of 2.3–6% assumed ads would hold still while sales quadrupled, so every product "failed" a cap while making money. Decide on profit and break-even TACoS instead.
