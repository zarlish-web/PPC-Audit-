Scale Insights: keep for now, cancel before Nov 17

We pay $310.40 a month for a plan that gives us far more than its price, and we only use it for the overnight pause. The real issue is that the pause misses 40% of our ad spend. Fix that now at no cost, move the pause in-house, then cancel.

Prepared from the Scale Insights owner account (Ecotero and Inspiratek logins), the Automated ASINs export, SellerBoard ad spend for Sep 15 – Oct 14, Scale Insights' documentation and the team's Slack history. This replaces the Oct 13 estimate in the thread.

Recommendation

  1. Don't downgrade. A cheaper tier saves only $80–$248 a month and would likely cut dayparting to 35, 20 or 5 ASINs.
  2. Enable automation on 24 high-spend ASINs now. Dayparting coverage goes from 60% to 82% of ad spend, at no extra cost.
  3. Move the overnight pause in-house by about Nov 12, run both for 3 nights, download the archived data, and cancel before the Nov 17 renewal. Saving: $310.40 a month, $3,725 a year.

Needed: Erik's approval for steps 2 and 3, and a date from Joseph for the in-house overnight pause.
