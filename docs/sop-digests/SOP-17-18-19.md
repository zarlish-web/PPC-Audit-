**Verdict / purpose.** The non-SP formats are **3 distinct products sold under 2 console labels**. This playbook operates all 3 against the **M6 structure targets: SP near 80%, SB 15-20%, SD 5-10% of format spend**. Each format has a different job:
- **SB** buys headline shelf on terms SP has already proven.
- **SBV** buys the same shopper cheaper whenever the CVR-adjusted inequality holds. It is the criteria authority's standard response to SP CPC pressure (Section 8.10, **S-F3**).
- **SD** buys pages and audiences and is the cheapest own-page defense surface.

All 3 are graded **click-attributed on the same economics chain as SP, never on view-attributed console numbers**. v2.0 expanded v1.0 from 6 to 13 procedures, from 7 to 12 failure modes, from 1 to 4 worked examples, and from a 6-row local verdict list to the full 25-string closed vocabulary. It also adds Elements 11 and 12, which no other shipped SOP in the 23-document library carried before this build.

**Why one playbook (1.1):** the 3 formats share 4 mechanisms: 1 measurement doctrine (click attribution only), 1 creative precondition gate (no launch on a placeholder asset), 1 monthly governance check (the M6 split), and 1 event overlay. Splitting them would violate anti-drift rule 3. Each section can split into a standalone SOP later without renumbering.

## 2. Scope and boundaries

**The three products (1.2):**
- **SB (#17)**: 3 declared roles, each its own campaign class:
  - (a) **brand-defense headline** on own brand terms
  - (b) **category headline** on terms already proven in SP exact
  - (c) **store spotlight** for family cross-sell, graded on the basket read

  **SB never discovers.** A discovery term in an SB list is F1, because the SP discovery layers (SOP-13, SOP-14, SOP-15) do the same job at lower CPC with better negation control.
- **SBV (#18)**: the arbitrage instrument. Entry/exit test is the inequality **SBV effective CPC x (SP CVR / SBV CVR) < SP exact CPC**. Doctrine basis: Strategic Doctrine v1.0 Section 7. Numeric trigger: criteria authority Section 8.10 row S-F3.
- **SD (#19)**: 2 products under 1 label:
  - **contextual page targeting**: own-page defense at floor; competitor pages under SOP-16 flag logic, unchanged
  - **audiences**: views and purchases remarketing, plus the rare event-tied in-market buy

  **SD Performance+** is permitted only under the P9 guardrail cycle.

## 4. Procedures

### P1. SB brand-defense headline operations (weekly, 20 min)
1. Ads Console > Sponsored Brands, filter to T3 C1 objective = **Defensive**. Cross-check against #26 defense-set membership. If a campaign is absent from the #26 set, route it to #26 the same day.
2. Keyword lists are **exact-only own brand terms**. Verify against T3 group **C5** that every keyword carries the brand root. Remove generic terms in the staged set; a proven category term re-routes to P2.
3. Bid posture is floor. Read click-attributed ACOS against the **Defensive band 10-15%** (Section 3 F1 "Objective Max-ACOS bands"; Section 6 **O6**).
   - Inside band with position held: no change.
   - **Above 15%** with position held: 1 ladder step down (L2 mechanics owned by #28), staged per #38.
4. Position evidence: headline position share for brand terms from the weekly SB report. A slipping share with in-band ACOS restores 1 step (mirrors **S-D2**). Share bands do not exist (A-6), so the read is direction only (rising/flat/slipping **across 2 reads**), never a level.
5. Walls per #29: brand terms negated out of generic discovery. The wall state field in T3 C5 reads **STEERING** for each defense term.
6. T6 prediction with review date on every change; the pass opens with V5 scoring.

### P2. SB category headline operations (weekly, 30 min)
1. Candidacy: a term enters only with proven SP exact history, meaning qualified CVR and CPC evidence at the G2 bar (**≥100 clicks** for the CVR read) and a Conversions or post-objective state in T2. Unproven or non-exact entry = F1.
2. Creation: campaign per syntax family, exact-only list, headline and logo assets from the register, landing per the asset plan. Name per v4.0 Section 4.2 (restated in SOP-12 v2.0 Appendix A) with the SB type segment. Non-parsing name = naming defect; renames ride alone.
3. Walls per #29 pre-loaded in the same session: brand terms excluded from category SB; graduated-term steering mirrored so category SB and SP exact never fight for the same query without declared roles.
4. Bid: seed against the SP benchmark CPC for the same term from T2 group **K5**. Read weekly on click-attributed ACOS vs the SB objective band. SB is graded on its own band plus **NTB depth, vCTR, completion** per T3 group **C4** (Data Architecture v1.0 Section 7, "never judged on SP metrics").
5. Exit: **NTB% and ACOS both below bar for 2 validated weeks** → exit or re-creative per **S-F2**. Step down or pause with S-F2 in the reason string.
6. T6 prediction; T3 registry updated (type counts in T2 **K8** pick up SB presence per term).

### P3. SB store-spotlight (weekly, 15 min)
1. Spotlight drives to Store pages for family cross-sell. Grade on the basket: units per order and store dwell from the SB report, plus click-attributed sales vs its own band. Never last-click ACOS alone (carried from v1.0 P1.1c).
2. Store-page readiness is a pending fold-in on the Ledger row for #17. Interim check is binary: the Store page for the family exists and is current per the brand owner. Stale or missing parks the spotlight.
3. Stage per #38 with T6 predictions. Spotlight has the **lowest budget priority of the 3 SB roles** and is first trimmed in a P11 rebalance.

### P4. SBV candidacy and probe entry (per candidacy, 25 min)
1. Candidacy requires all 3:
   - proven SP exact (qualified CVR and CPC history at G2)
   - APPROVED video in the register
   - clean gate state (no event freeze, fresh economics chain)
2. Screen with **S-F3**: head-term condition "**SP exact CPC >=1.5x ceiling**" together with "**SBV CPC <=0.6x SP exact CPC**". Section 9 rule **L8** defines the substitution lever. At candidacy the SBV CPC is the console's suggested-bid estimate; the definitive test waits for the probe.
3. Open the probe: dated, budget-capped, 1 term, exact, video creative, with cap and stop-loss date in the creation set. No inequality verdict on day-3 optics.
4. Register the SP and SBV instances as a **declared cross-format pair** in T3 C5 with roles, so the pair is never read as duplicates (1 term posture per L8).
5. T6 entry: predicted SBV CVR band, click quota, cap, stop-loss date, review date.

### P5. SBV inequality test and arbitrage registration (per test, 20 min)
1. Test only at **≥100 probe clicks** (G2). Below the bar at the stop-loss date: close as unqualified, re-park.
2. Compute adjusted SBV cost = **SBV effective CPC x (SP CVR / SBV CVR)** and compare it to SP exact CPC on the same term. Basis: Doctrine Section 7 and Economics Worked-Example Set case **E6**. Adjust before comparing; never compare raw CPCs across formats.
3. Decision tree:

| Branch | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| OPEN | Adjusted SBV cost < SP exact CPC AND S-F3 held at entry | Verdict **ENTER SBV ARBITRAGE**; #12 executes the SP-exact ladder step-down dated with the SBV go-live; the arbitrage record opens with both inputs, the adjusted cost, and a dated re-test |
| CLOSED at entry | Adjusted ≥ SP CPC with qualified data | Probe → **PAUSED-PENDING (REASON + RE-ENTRY)**; re-entry = materially lower SBV CPC or higher SBV CVR; no SP step-down |
| Unqualified | Data unqualified at stop-loss | Probe closes, spend recorded, re-park; re-entry needs a new asset or a changed market read |

4. Re-test interval is a dated field. No value exists in the criteria file (**A-3**). Interim: the PPC Lead sets it at registration, with the account's recorded case (**6-week re-test**) as the working reference. Re-testing is mandatory.
5. T6 entry with the full computation in the reason string; human-mandatory confirmation before the pair deploys.

### P6. SBV re-test, step-down, exit (per re-test, 15 min)
1. Pull current SBV CPC and CVR at qualified volume and recompute as in P5.
2. Still open: append the pass to the re-test log, set a new date, change nothing else.
3. Reversed: SBV bid steps down 1 L2 ladder step, or pauses if the reversal margin exceeds a step's reach. The record closes with both tests on file. SP exact continues untouched; any SP restore is a #12 decision. Creative fatigue (completion rate falling) → exit or refresh per **S-F4** even while the CPC side holds.
4. Past re-test date = F3; re-test the day it is found.

### P7. SD contextual (weekly, 30 min)
1. Own-page defense: own-ASIN contextual at floor bids, permanently. The read is presence (impressions on own pages) at the **Defensive band 10-15% ACOS**. A #26 P4 incursion signal escalates the floor 1 step the same session.
2. Competitor pages: SOP-16's flag workflow runs unchanged (open only under a weakness flag, per-target reads, rotate on reversal, register failed targets). Verdict string **OPEN / ROTATE / EXIT CONQUEST**. #16 is upstream-blocked on the competitor depth feed (Ledger dependency **D4**), so competitor contextual stays structural until it lands; own-page defense is unaffected.
3. Blended contextual ACOS is never read; every verdict is per target class.
4. Grading: click-attributed CPA vs ceiling, where ceiling = contribution margin $ (**F1**: "Sets the CPA ceiling (= margin $)").

### P8. SD audiences (weekly, 25 min)
1. Entry classes:
   - **Views remarketing**: highest-yield entry class.
   - **Purchases-based**: complements and repurchase timing, capped.
   - **In-market/lifestyle conquest**: rare and only event-tied per #32.

   Postures per Doctrine Section 8; entry economics per **S-F5**.
2. Pool-size gate: enter only above the pool floor (trickle pool = F6). No floor value exists (**A-1**). Interim: PPC Lead human-mandatory sign-off on pool size. Calibration reference (not a threshold): the account's recorded case, a **4,100-member views pool** admitted.
3. Lookback windows are named in Doctrine Section 8 as doctrine only; decision-grade values are **A-2**. AMC-built audiences (cart abandoners, high-intent viewers) arrive from #20 and pass the same pool gate.
4. Grading is mode-aware (mandatory):
   - **CPC mode**: click-attributed CPA vs ceiling (**S-F5**).
   - **vCPM mode**: NTB% and assisted conversions, never raw ACOS (**S-F6**).

   View-attributed numbers are recorded in reporting with their label and discarded from decisions (F2).
5. Every scale step re-checks marginal ACOS. A step is legal only while **marginal ACOS ≤ 1.5x average** (Section 4 rule **X11**, Section 10 rule **M7**).

### P9. SD Performance+ guardrail cycle (weekly, 20 min)
1. Opt-in only, and only for remarketing audiences, with a strict return floor. Never for open prospecting on a mid-size budget (Doctrine Section 8). Numeric guardrails (cap sizing, return floor, expansion tolerance) do not exist (**A-4**). Interim: an explicit dollar cap set at entry, human-mandatory.
2. Exclusion lists load at entry: own catalog and previously failed targets.
3. The weekly expansion review is the gate artifact: read the expansion log, list what the mode added, check the week's click-attributed CPA vs ceiling.
   - Missing review = F4.
   - A second consecutive miss escalates.
4. Suspension: a cap breach or CPA above ceiling suspends the campaign in the staged set. Re-entry needs a reloaded exclusion list and a re-set cap.

### P10. Weekly format read pass (Monday cascade, 45 min)
1. T0 and T1 before T3 (Data Architecture Section 2 rule 1); otherwise the pass is invalid.
2. V5-score due T6 format rows first.
3. Filter T3 to Type in SB, SBV, SD. Check gates before metrics: eligibility, truncation, event flag, manifest status.
4. Read each row against its own objective band (T3 C4) and verify the attribution label on every SD/SBV number.
5. Routing tree (5 branches):

| Branch | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gate failing | Execute the gate lever (budget on truncation, halt on eligibility); defer the metric verdict |
| 2 | SB row failing S-F2 (2 validated weeks) | Exit or re-creative per P2.5 |
| 3 | SBV row at/past re-test date | P6 now |
| 4 | SD row citing a view-attributed number | F2; re-issue click-attributed first |
| 5 | Clean and in band | No change |

6. Stage per #38 with verdict/scenario ID; deploy Tuesday; consume T+1; mismatches → F10 same day.

### P11. Monthly format-split check (first Monday, 60 min)
1. Compute spend shares for SP, SB, SD from the **A4 Structure targets** tab vs **M6: SP ~80%, SB 15-20%, SD 5-10%** (Section 10 rule M6; the same splits appear in Section 3 family **F8**).
2. Deviations are findings to explain, never errors to auto-correct (M6's own clause). Drift tree:
   - **All in band:** record the check; no rebalance.
   - **Above band top:** the format re-justifies with its marginal yield read (click-attributed contribution of the marginal dollar vs the marginal SP dollar). A losing read stages a rebalance at keyword/campaign level, trimming by verdict with the spotlight class first. Never a blanket budget shift.
   - **Below band floor:** a finding with a named cause (asset absence, closed arbitrages, paused audiences) and a dated fill-or-explain owner. A silent shortfall = F7.
3. Split changes are deliberate staged deployments with T6 predictions. F7 = drift on automation defaults with no rebalance decision on file.

### P12. NTB reading rule (per read, 10 min)
1. The **January 2026 attribution tightening** re-baselined NTB rates account-wide (Doctrine Section 7). Compare NTB only within post-change windows; a trend across the boundary = F5.
2. NTB is a strategy read and an input to S-F1, S-F2, S-F6 grading. It is never a bid input unless a verdict names it (carried from v1.0 P5.2).

### P13. Event overlay (per #32 plan)
1. Tentpole headline positions (SB and SBV) deploy only per the #32 plan with its caps. A headline deployment in an armed window without a plan reference is the format-side twin of SOP-12's frozen-structure rule.
2. SD remarketing pools grow during windows. The post-window remarketing pass is the 1 deliberate settle-period action, named in the plan itself; it is not an exclusion breach (v1.0 P6.2).
3. Event weeks + **14 days following** are excluded from trend verdicts. Reads inside the window run against the event projection only.

### Worked-example fictional base (illustrative; not account data except where noted as carried account cases)
AOV $80.00, contribution $28.00/order, BE ACOS 35.0%, Target ACOS 17.5%, Max Acceptable 26.3%.
- **WE-1 (carried account case from v1.0):** SP exact CPC $2.10 at CVR 5.0%; Max Profitable CPC = 28.00 x 0.050 = $1.40; ratio 1.50 (meets ≥1.5x at equality). Suggested SBV CPC $0.95 ≤ 0.6 x 2.10 = $1.26. Probe: SBV CPC $0.95, CVR 3.2%. Adjusted = 0.95 x 5.0/3.2 = $1.48 vs $2.10, a 29.3% discount → OPEN, 6-week re-test. Week 6: SBV CPC $1.40, CVR 3.0%. Adjusted = $2.33 > $2.10 → REVERSED; step down; record closes.
- **WE-2 (carried account case):** Views pool 4,100. Console view-attributed CPA $9.12 (trap). Click-attributed: $178.20 / 9 orders = $19.80 vs $28.00 ceiling. Marginal: prior $118.80 spend / $486.00 sales; now $178.20 / $648.00. Marginal ACOS = 59.40/162.00 = 36.7%; average 27.5%; 1.5x = 41.3%; 1 budget step legal.
- **WE-3:** Performance+ cap $150.00/wk. Week 1 review skipped (F4); week 2 spend $212.00 (41.3% breach); CPA $22.40 → $31.50 (12.5% above ceiling) → SUSPENDED.
- **WE-4:** Prior month SP 78.4% / SB 16.2% / SD 5.4% (carried v1.0 case, in band). This month $24,600.00 total: SP $18,257.00 (74.2%), SB $5,314.00 (21.6%), SD $1,029.00 (4.2%). Marginal SB $1,000 → $310.00 contribution vs SP $460.00. Trim $1,400.00/month from spotlight → SB 15.9%, SP 79.9%. SD shortfall cause: 2 closed arbitrages reduced SBV-adjacent SD retargeting.
