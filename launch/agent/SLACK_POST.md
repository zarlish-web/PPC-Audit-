# Slack post format

House style for the post:
- Human tone.
- Opens with "Update from my side".
- No tables. One short line per check, numbered 1 to 8.
- Figures come from Sellerboard.
- It ends with the asks, the questions that need the owner, and the link to the full page.
- Keep it under about 200 words. The detail lives on the shareable page.

## Template

```
Update from my side, <Product> for <Day Date>

1. Sales and profit: <units> units, $<sales>, net <+/-$>. Ads $<ads> (TACoS <x>%). <best/worst size line>.
2. Parent check: <matches / differs by …>.
3. Ad efficiency: SP ACoS <x>%, brand ACoS <y>% vs break-even <be>%. <size share line>.
4. Stop-loss: <campaigns past 30 clicks over the line, or "nothing over the line">. Next to reach 30: <…>.
5. Waste: <terms or campaigns at 10+ clicks and no order, or "none">.
6. Ranks: <head-term moves; guard status>.
7. Stock: <SKUs out or low; total cover vs the boat>.
8. Engine: <changes, owner moves matched to decisions, anything unexplained>.

Asks: <1–3 decisions for the owner>
Full page: <shareable link>
```
