## Verdict

The plan is sound in direction. Ranking first at today's prices, using the light push as the pilot that sets the push budget, committing stock on the conservative line, and testing every ad type are all right for a launch.

It needs revision before it is stored in Target Setting. Three things matter most:

1. **The push cost is overstated by far more than "~2×".**
   - The model books $1,173 a day of rank spend in October and $2,247 / $5,555 a day of total ads in November / December.
   - Live spend is about $290–435 a day across all ad types. October is about 3–4× live, December about 19×.
   - Step 2's budgets are $1,913 a day, but 19 Oct spent $435.
   - The light push read (CL-PU8) will set the real number. The tracker is reporting it daily.
2. **The size split runs opposite to live sales.**
   - The plan is Queen-led: 46% Queen and 30% King of units, and Queen's push is valued highest ($611k).
   - Live sales since ads started are King 9.3 a day against Queen 3.5 (2.6×).
   - King is the binding stock size. At the plan's pace King runs out ~Dec 13 and again ~Jan 12, before Fastest lands Jan 23.
   - Re-check the 17,175-unit conservative split by size before the ~Oct 23 order.
3. **Queen cannot move yet because of how it is set up.**
   - Queen is on a 50% light push, but only 7 of its 40 push keywords sit on a rank exact.
   - Its biggest keyword, "cooling sheets queen", is held on profit by the heads rule.
   - Result: median sponsored position #7 (slot 1 in 4% of hours) against King's #1 (37%), and organic #68–76.
   - Sleephoria is ranking-bidding the same keyword this week (20 clicks, 3 orders at +145–160% top-of-search). CooLinens got 16 clicks and 0 orders. That is the opposite of "CooLinens on the higher slot".
