# CooLinens Cooling Sheets 4PC — Historical evidence summary

Companion to `CooLinens_Cooling_Sheets_4PC_Historical_Analysis_v1.xlsx` (numbers, by tab) and `CooLinens_Cooling_Sheets_4PC_Launch_Strategy_v2.xlsx` (the plan). Prepared 8 Oct 2026.

## Findings


### How to read this

Two benchmark products: Sleephoria Cooling Sheets 4PC (same COGS, colours, sizes and today's price as CooLinens → the economic twin) and Decolure Cooling Sheets 4PC (~$38 microfiber tier, launched Oct 2023 → the long history). Periods: full history from each launch; current P_cur = 1 Jul–7 Oct 2026 vs P_ly = same window 2025 (and 2024 for Decolure); trailing 30 days vs same 30 days last year; Q4-24 vs Q4-25. Tab names in [brackets] hold the numbers.

### 1. Economics — price tier decides profit, not PPC skill

• Sleephoria: $800k sales, NP $135.7k (17.0%, $11.16/unit) since Jul 2025; L12M TACoS 9.6%. Decolure: $997k lifetime sales, NP $134 (≈ $0/unit); lifetime PPC ACoS 44.2% vs ~28% break-even; LTSF + storage $70k. Amazon fees are the same dollars per unit ($19.1–19.2) for both, so the $65–78 price flows to profit and the $38 price does not. [Monthly_Business, DC_Monthly_YoY, Period_Compare]
• Implication: CooLinens sits on Sleephoria's economics (break-even ACoS ~31–38% by size at today's price). Below ~$60 ASP the Decolure outcome applies.

### 2. Current period vs same period last year

• Decolure, Jul 1–Oct 7: 2024 → 2025 → 2026 total sales $197.9k → $107.5k → $26.2k; sessions 123k → 75k → 19k; PPC spend $35.4k → $7.6k → $7.3k; ACoS 41.2% → 50.4% → 55.5%; TACoS 17.9% → 7.1% → 28.0%; net profit $13.9k → $14.6k → −$9.2k. 2025 spent 78% less than 2024 and made MORE profit; 2026 spent the same as 2025 and lost $23.8k of profit (stockouts, removal order, ranks gone). [DC_Monthly_YoY]
• Decolure trailing 30d: 2024 $37.5k sales / TACoS 20.9% → 2025 $9.7k / 10.9% → 2026 $7.1k / 42.3%. Q4-24 → Q4-25: spend −91.5%, sales −78%, loss shrank −$9.6k → −$1.5k. [DC_Monthly_YoY]
• Sleephoria has no like-for-like year (Jul–Sep 2025 was pre-ads Vine: $1.9–4.5k/month). Best comparison: launch quarter Oct–Dec 2025 $2,360/day total sales at TACoS 16.7% (ACoS 41.5%, CPC $3.99) → best stretch Feb–May 2026 $2,861/day at TACoS 5.0% (ACoS 24.5%, CPC $2.15) → P_cur $1,780/day at TACoS 9.8% → trailing 30d $1,663/day at TACoS 13.0%, CVR 8.5%. [Period_Compare]

### 3. Launch history — what it cost to get ranked, and what happened after

• Sleephoria (3 Oct 2025): 13 of 14 head terms reached organic top 10 within 6–20 days. Spend ON each term at entry was modest: cooling sheets queen $40.9/day (7.8 clicks, 1.3 orders/day, top 10 in 9 days), cooling sheets king size $30.2/day, king size cooling sheets $18.6/day, cooling sheets full size $12.4/day — the account total was ~$600/day across ~100 single-keyword exacts (with PBDD tailwind). [SL_Rank_vs_Spend]
• Launch bids were ~2× what rank needed: late Nov 2025 CPC fell $4.21 → $2.48 (−40%) and ACoS 36% → 21.5% while total sales held $19–27k/week. [SL_Weekly_Timeline]
• Decolure (Nov 2023): $9.5k spend in month 1, then cut to $8–71/week Dec–Mar → weekly sales collapsed $7.5k → $0.4–1.0k with no organic floor; the Mar-24 restart + spring took it back to $17k/week in 4 weeks. Starving a new listing straight after launch erases it. [DC_Timeline]
• Re-entering top 10 later costs far less than the first entry: Sleephoria queen re-entered top 10 on 7 Mar 2026 on $19.9/day (8 clicks, 1.4 orders/day) and held ≤ 15 for 57 days. [SL_Rank_vs_Spend]

### 4. Keywords and syntax — consistent winners vs consistent waste

• Head term: 'cooling sheets' ranked #3–5 Feb–Apr 2026 with < $0.10/day on that exact term — rank followed velocity on the size terms. Bidding it directly ran 117% ACoS (Sleephoria Oct 2025); Decolure 'cooling sheets' exact $5.2k at 40.5% = consistent waste. [SL_Consistency, DC_Consistency]
• Size groups (Sleephoria search terms Jan–Oct 2026): Queen $9.0k at 30.6% ACoS / CPC $2.20; King $9.7k at 36.5% / $3.59 (break-even 32.8%); Full $7.3k at 35.1%; Cal King $2.7k at 31.6%; Twin $1.5k at 28.2%. Twin was the only non-brand CONSISTENT WINNER; the big size terms are VOLATILE — profitable Feb–May and Aug, losing in Jun and Oct. [SL_PPC_Splits, SL_Consistency]
• Consistent waste (Sleephoria): king cooling sheets $1,251 @ 60%, cooling bed sheets $635 @ 42%, cooling sheets full $472, cooling sheets king $376, cooling king sheets $239, cooling bed sheets queen $169 @ 57%, cooling sheets for hot sleepers $156 @ 285%. Decolure: 56 consistently wasteful exact targets cost $74k pre-2025; 'cooling sheets queen' $2,487 at 45.9% was profitable 0 of 7 months. [SL_Consistency, DC_Consistency]
• Branded/defensive is the only consistently profitable engine: Sleephoria branded terms $10.7k at 14.1% ACoS / 20% CVR, branded campaign profitable 13 of 13 months. [SL_Consistency]
• Market-level conversion (SQP 12 months): queen/king modifier variants convert 4.4–6.1% (queen bed sheets cooling 6.1%, queen sheets cooling 5.0%, queen cooling sheets 5.0%) vs 3.35% 'cooling sheets queen', 3.0% 'cooling sheets king size' and 1.55% for the head term. Sleephoria under-indexes exactly there (purchase share ÷ impression share 0.42–0.47). [SQP_Funnel]

### 5. Match type, targeting type, ad product, placement

• Sleephoria: exact keyword campaigns = 66–91% of spend every month; auto ran 3–11% ACoS, phrase/broad (from Aug 2026) 13.5–21.7% — efficient but never funded beyond $0.4k/month. PAT, category, SB and SD were never tested. [SL_PPC_Splits]
• Decolure (pre-2025 Sellerboard + CC): exact never reached break-even in any quarter (36–61%); SD auto 20–33% and SD product-similar 26.2% were the cheapest formats; PAT 28–47%; SB keyword 53–94% (e.g. 'cool sheets' SB $2,100 @ 90.5%); broad 209%. Auto at $0.6 CPC in 2026 bought paid sales (CVR 3.6–4.7%) but not organic rank. [DC_PPC_Splits]
• Placement (Sleephoria measured May–Sep 2026): Top of Search 24.6–41.6% ACoS at the highest CPC; Product Pages 20–27%; Rest of Search 13.5–27%; Off-Amazon $661 with 0 orders. Decolure's Sep-2026 TOS modifiers of 168–174% pushed CPC to $2.43–2.60 and ACoS to 76% with no rank response. [SL_PPC_Splits, DC_Timeline]
• Campaigns labelled 'Conversions' converted no better than 'Ranking' (Sleephoria P_cur 40.2% vs 32.6%; Decolure 73.8% vs 59.5%). Structure sprawl hurt: Sleephoria went from 14–23 campaigns/month to 128–145 in Aug–Sep 2026 with only 27–32 spending ≥ $50. [SL_Weekly_Timeline]

### 6. SKU / variation performance

• Colour demand (share of units, full history): Sleephoria Graphite 18.8%, Cool Grey 13.2%, Navy 9.4%, White 8.0%, Light Blue 7.8%; Decolure Graphite 20.6%, Cool Grey 15.8%, Midnight Black 12.9%, Regal Purple 11.5%. Size: Sleephoria Queen 42 / King 26 / Full 15 / Twin 9 / Cal King 8 (King 28% and Cal King 11% recently); Decolure Queen 48 / King 27. [Size_Colour_Rollups]
• Hero-only advertising distorts SKU metrics: Sleephoria Queen Graphite, Queen Cool Grey and Full Cool Grey took 60% of ad spend for 17% of units; their unit-session CVR is 2.4–3.4% and NP/unit −$1.8 to −$19.8, vs 6–7% CVR and $16–21/unit for unadvertised colours (Navy, Light Blue, King Light Grey, Queen White). Shoppers click the hero ad and buy another colour — judge colours on unit share and NP with ads spread by units ($10–15/unit for every colour). [Ad_Concentration, SKU_Scorecard_SL]
• Sleephoria ad results by advertised child: Queen Graphite $30.0k spend / 35.8% ACoS / 12.0% CVR; King Graphite $16.1k / 41.8% / 13.6%; Full Cool Grey $8.7k / 35.6%; Cal King Graphite $3.5k / 34.3% / 15.2%; Twin Graphite $2.2k / 31.6%. [SL_Advertised_Child]

### 7. Inventory — the single biggest cause of lost sales and rank

• Sleephoria May–Jul 2026 was a near whole-brand stockout (20 of 21 tracked SKUs at 0 by 5 Jul; Queen Graphite 0 from 24 May–9 Jul, King Graphite 1 Jun–10 Jul, King White ~95 days). Brand sales/day −42% ($1,994 → $1,155); ACoS 27.9% → 34.7%; 'cooling sheets' rank 12 → 43 then unranked; 'cooling sheets queen' 18 → 66 → out of the top 100 for 28 days; market purchases in June (peak month) went to competitors (SQP purchase share 5.8% Apr → 0.1% Jun). Queen had still not recovered by October (33–45). [OOS_Impact, SQP_Share_vs_Rank]
• 55% of Sleephoria's attributable spend pointed at Graphite children and was never re-pointed to in-stock colours during the OOS; King-advertised campaigns ran 76% ACoS / 6.6% CVR in June. [SL_Advertised_Child]
• Decolure Queen Graphite (#1 SKU) OOS 27 Jul–2 Sep and 9 Sep → now: brand $/day −46%, sessions −50%, queen ranks 42 → ~125. Rank decay with spend cut + OOS was 35–45 positions/month vs 3 months to double rank after a spend-only cut. [OOS_Impact, DC_Rank_vs_Spend]
• Stock cover 14 days before observed stockouts: median 17.5 days (25th pct 11.9, n = 27) → a 21-day switch/replenish trigger. Right now: Sleephoria King Graphite OOS since 6 Oct, Queen & King Cool Grey OOS since July with nothing inbound; Decolure #1 SKU at 0. [OOS_Events]

### 8. Seasonality and demand

• SQP (45-keyword balanced panel, Oct 2025–Sep 2026), index vs 12-month average: Oct 0.76 · Nov 0.66 · Dec 0.62 · Jan 0.55 (trough) · Feb 0.59 · Mar 0.94 · Apr 1.05 · May 1.21 · Jun 1.80 (peak) · Jul 1.65 · Aug 1.22 · Sep 0.93. Market purchases follow the same curve (Jan 0.48, Jun 1.87). The 15 Oct–31 Jan launch window = ~35% of peak searches and ~30% of peak purchases. [Seasonality]
• Year-on-year demand: Feb-25 → Feb-26 +38% and Mar-24 → Mar-26 +61% on the same 25 keywords (DataDive snapshots vs SQP); early Oct-2026 is running flat vs Oct-2025. Mix: Queen's share of cooling-sheet searches rising (23% → 26%); 'for hot sleepers' phrasing collapsed (4.6% → ~1%). [Seasonality]
• Rival ad intensity is highest Jan, Aug and Sep (ad share of placement score 0.52–0.59) and lowest in June (0.35). [Comp_Trends]

### 9. Major rises and declines — and why

• Rises: Sleephoria Oct 2025 launch push (confirmed); Sleephoria Jan–Mar 2026 best run (spring demand + exact size terms at ~$2.3 CPC; TACoS ~4%); Decolure Mar–Aug 2024 (PPC restart + season; Aug-24 record $84.6k at 39.8% ACoS — volume, no profit); Decolure Apr-2025 price cut $37.6 → $26.1 tripled units with flat spend, and restoring price in June kept revenue with the best profit months; Sleephoria Aug 2026 re-push after restock. [SL_Weekly_Timeline, DC_Timeline]
• Declines: seasonal Dec–Jan troughs (both products); Decolure H2-2025 = season end + 70% spend cut in Aug (decline began 14 Jul with spend unchanged); Sleephoria May–Jul 2026 = stockout (confirmed); Sleephoria Sep–Oct 2026 = season end + price now ~27% above launch (CVR 11.6% → 9.2%, likely) + King Graphite OOS; Decolure Aug–Oct 2026 = stockouts + removal order + unprofitable TOS push. [SL_Weekly_Timeline, DC_Timeline]
• CORRECTION to Strategy v1: Decolure's session collapse started in Jul 2025, before Sleephoria's ads began (3 Oct 2025), and the two sold at different price tiers; in Mar-2026 both grew together. Evidence that Sleephoria cannibalised Decolure is WEAK (low confidence). The CooLinens–Sleephoria overlap is still a real risk, but because they are the same product at the same price — not because of the Decolure precedent. [DC_Timeline]
• Unexplained: Decolure's Mar-2026 rank recovery (89 → 20 on 'cooling sheets queen' with $131 spend) and sessions spike; Sleephoria's Mar–Apr 2026 browser-session spike. Needs Business Reports to resolve.

### 10. Price, deals and events

• Price is a strong lever in this category: Decolure −30% price → 4× weekly units (Apr 2025); Love's cabin −24% → ~3× volume; Bedsure PureWoven Cooling $114.99 → $89.99 → sales 2.3×. Sleephoria's +$5 steps in Aug 2026 (to $77.58 ASP) coincided with CVR falling 11.6% → 9.2%. CooLinens at $79.99 Queen sits at the low end of the premium cluster ($79–89.99). [Comp_Trends, SL_Weekly_Timeline]
• Deals buy volume, not profit: Decolure Dec-24 promo $9.1k → best CVR (12.4%) but −$5.0k month; PBDD 2026 account read: TACoS 7.7% with a deal vs 17% without. [DC_Timeline]

### 11. Competitors (ASINsight 20 rivals, 61,173 keyword rows; position scores Jan–Sep 2026; DataDive 2024–2026)

• Cooling-specific terms are only ~9% of rival traffic (1.0M of 10.7M weekly); generic size heads ('queen sheet set', 'king size sheets set', 'queen sheets') are 42%; bamboo terms 33% (not addressable — CooLinens/Sleephoria fabric is not bamboo). [Comp_KW_Universe]
• Organic leaders are bamboo sets (Bedsure PureWoven #2 on 'cooling sheets', 456 organic top-10 keywords; Love's cabin 8% ad share). Premium cooling-tech brands buy their position (REST 78% ad share, 14 organic top-10 keywords; Breescape relies on 1,163 SBV keywords) and Coolmax/polyester 'cooling' has decayed since 2024 (Degrees of Comfort BSR 2.5k → 38.7k). [Comp_Scorecard, Comp_Trends]
• Winning launch pattern: KRIMANO (listed 14 Oct 2025 at $79.99–89.99, 4.5–4.6★) — ad share 0.26 → 0.76 by month 5, then 0.43 by month 11 as organic score grew ~600×; now 88 organic top-10 keywords and still gaining (+123% traffic in late Sep). It bought SP on generic size heads, not only cooling terms. Failed patterns: ads-only with no organic conversion (KARISSA MAE, BEDELITE > 80% ad share, organic falling); under-funded premium tech (Elegear); price-led tech (REST, Breescape). [Comp_Launch_Playbooks]
• 35 cooling terms are 'ad-dominated' (≤ 1 rival in organic top 10 but ≥ 10 advertising): full/twin/cal king cooling, cooling fitted sheet, cooling bedding, cold sheets … — top slots there are reachable with paid traffic. [Comp_Launch_KW_Top150]

## What this changes in the launch strategy


### Confirmed from v1 (evidence now stronger)

1. Lead with the Queen modifier cluster on single-keyword exact (cooling sheets queen, queen sheets cooling, queen cooling sheets, cooling bed sheets queen, queen size cooling sheets, cooling queen sheets). Evidence: highest market CVR (5–6%), Sleephoria under-indexes there, Queen share of demand rising, Queen = 41% of CooLinens stock.
2. Do not buy the head term ('cooling sheets', 'cooling bed sheets') for rank — capped probe only. Evidence: rank followed size-term velocity; direct bids ran 117% (SL) and 40.5% consistent waste (BM).
3. Per-term budgets of ~$12–50/day are enough to enter the top 10 (Sleephoria Oct 2025 entry spend). The v1 Batch-1 sizing (~$49/day on 'cooling sheets queen', ~$175/day total post-declaration) sits in that range. Expect a slower climb than Sleephoria's 6–20 days: launch-window demand is ~35% of peak and there is no PBDD tailwind.
4. Gate King terms (CPC $3.59, 36.5% ACoS vs 32.8% break-even at SL; 79–96% at BM). Twin and Cal King rank cheaply but are Sleephoria-held.

### Changed or added by the historical analysis

5. CORRECTION: drop the 'Decolure lost 85% of sessions because of Sleephoria' rationale (decline started before Sleephoria's ads; low confidence). Keep SOP-33 arbitration because CooLinens = Sleephoria in product and price.
6. Preferred / backup per size (Preferred_Backup tab): Queen Graphite → Cool Grey; King Graphite → Cool Grey; Cal King Graphite → Midnight Black; Full Cool Grey → Graphite; Twin Graphite → Navy. Switch the advertised child when preferred cover < 21 days (trailing-7d velocity) or below the unit floor (Queen 76, King 49, Full 16, Cal King/Twin 15) with nothing receivable in 10 days; switch back at ≥ 45 days cover. Sleephoria never re-pointed during its OOS and lost the season.
7. Re-order now for the Mar–Jul peak: CooLinens has no inbound. Queen Graphite 373 units ≈ 69–103 days at launch velocity but June demand runs ~3× the launch window → stock-out risk lands exactly on peak (the Sleephoria failure). Twin (41/35 units) and Cal King (66/42) are thin from day 1.
8. Taper faster once rank holds: Sleephoria cut CPC 40% after reaching rank with no sales loss. After 2 consecutive weeks at target rank, step bids down 15–25% per cycle (SOP limits) toward max-profitable CPC.
9. Seasonal budget shape: hold a floor through Dec–Jan (Sleephoria at $53/day in Jan lost queen 67 / king-size 101; Decolure's Dec-23 cut erased it) — ≥ $70–100/day on the Queen hero exacts — then plan a SECOND sized push in Feb–Mar, ahead of the Apr–Jul peak (demand nearly doubles in March; Sleephoria's best economics were Feb–May at TACoS 5%).
10. Phase 2 discovery (auto close-match, phrase) is efficient but builds sales, not rank (SL auto 3–11%, phrase/broad 13–22%; BM auto at $0.6 CPC bought paid sales only). Open it as a harvest surface when SOP-22 P7 allows; do not expect rank from it. Keep broad tightly capped (BM 209%).
11. Add a capped test of generic size heads in Batch 2 (KRIMANO pattern; Decolure 'queen size sheet set' exact was a consistent winner at 26.3%): queen sheet set, queen sheets, sheets queen size bed set — capped, Queen Graphite, down-only.
12. Branded defence from week 2–3: as soon as 'coolinens' searches appear in search terms, add SP branded exact (Sleephoria's branded campaign was profitable 13 of 13 months at ~14% ACoS). SB brand formats only after Phase 2 (SOP-22).
13. Placements: start all modifiers at 0 (SOP); never run TOS +150% without CVR evidence (Decolure Sep-26: +168–174% → 76% ACoS, no rank). Exclude Off-Amazon ($661, 0 orders). Product pages were not worse than TOS at Sleephoria (20–27% vs 24–42%) — test before blanket-zeroing PP on CooLinens.
14. Keep structure tight: ≤ 20–25 campaigns in the first 8 weeks, each with a declared objective; the 'Conversions' label did not convert better at either brand.
15. Price & deals: hold $79.99 during the push (no stacked price/image changes — G9 and Sleephoria Aug-26). Use the BFCM deal as a velocity/rank lever and budget it as below-break-even volume, not profit.
16. Listing: reversed-order Queen terms lose clicks (click share ÷ impression share 0.62–0.75 at SL) and Sleephoria leaks at cart → purchase → make 'Queen' + 'cooling sheets' visible in the main image/title and keep every Queen colour in stock (cart → purchase leaks when the chosen colour is unavailable).
17. Competitor pressure points for later phases: PAT/SD product-similar was the cheapest Decolure format (26%) — use the v1 PAT shortlist (REST, Breescape, Cozy Bliss, Elegear, DOWNCOOL, KARISSA MAE) in Phase 3–4; avoid low-price/huge-review ASINs.
