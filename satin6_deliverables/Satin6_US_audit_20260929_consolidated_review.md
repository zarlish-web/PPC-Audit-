### 4.4 Bids and placements on ranking campaigns

**Where the rejected cuts come from.** Every ranking-term cut both reviews reject cites the "Maintenance Profit Control" canvas, class HARD (operator 2026-09-24/25), "−25% off the top-of-search price". So does the $20 → $10 budget cut on "king size silk sheet set". They aren't the engine's pricing logic; they are a class setting someone chose. Fix the class (Appendix A), or the next run makes the same cuts.

1. **Price on the effective top-of-search bid** (base × (1 + modifier)), never on CPC. Both reviews agree.
2. **Never lower a ranking term's top-of-search price as a side effect.** Both reviews agree.
3. **Where product pages take more than 20% of a ranking campaign's clicks**, cut the base by up to 25% a write and raise the modifier so the top-of-search price holds (Review A; the bids document). Review B's concern is that cutting the base loses rest-of-search and product-page auctions, which carry 53% of the clicks. That's handled by the next rule, not by keeping the base high.
4. **Rest of search gets its own modifier once earned**: 15 or more clicks converting at or above the base rate. It pays best (18.7% ACoS on the 10–23 Sep settled window). Until then it stays at 0, so seq 94, 179 and 269 load.
5. **Pushes, when they come**, climb 20–30% a write within the engine's market bound (its top-of-search CPC + 15%), with the daily check below:
   - If top-of-search clicks run under 70% of plan for 3 non-deal days with budget intact, raise the next write.
   - If the budget runs out, raise the budget instead.
   - Don't cut the top-of-search price until 70% of the planned clicks arrive.

   Review B's rule (+25%, then +10% a week) is the same idea and a fair alternative.
6. **Bid rows above the engine's own market bound are held for Joseph:**
   - seq 45, 48, 49 and 50 ("queen size sheet set silk", $3.47 against a $2.89 bound);
   - seq 110 ("satin twin sheets set", $2.48 against $2.30);
   - 8 rows the engine held itself.
7. **Bidding stays "Dynamic bids – down only".** Review B notes that LEGACY_FOR_SALES is the same setting.
