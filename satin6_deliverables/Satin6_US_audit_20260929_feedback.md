## Summary

The audit runs S6 as a mature product in the maintenance lane. Its direction, cutting where rank isn't coming, is sound for a product that lost money after ads in 12 of the last 13 months. But its premises are wrong, much of it can't load, and it has no growth lever. Its cuts, colour swaps and new campaigns should not load as written.

**What the audit gets wrong:**
1. **The spend base isn't S6's.**
   - The engine reads $173 a day and a 24.7% TACoS. S6's own spend is $81–98 a day (14.0–15.0% TACoS).
   - The engine's list includes Satin Fitted and Bamboo campaigns, for example "BAMBOO-QUEEN-OLIVE SBV … bamboo sheets queen size".
   - It leaves out the S4-named LTSF auto ($26.81 a day) and a conquest campaign ($7.09 a day) that both advertise S6.
   - S6 is 2–3 points over its 12% ceiling, not double it.
2. **The margin is too high.** The engine uses $7.47 a unit (23.4% break-even). Sellerboard, per child, gives Queen Black $1.91 all-in and $4.61 excluding one-offs; the family is about 9% all-in and 18% excluding one-offs.
3. **The advertised children are wrong, and the colour swaps can't load.**
   - The engine's "hero" for Queen, Full and Twin is White by rule, although White sells 0, 6 and 1 units a month (the engine’s own stock table) against Black's 67–74, 24–25 and 12–13.
   - 68 of its 86 swaps break the size and colour rule. None of them loads, because the loader can't write product ads. The 323 renames would load.
4. **The forecast is 96% test artifact.** 122.8 of the 128.3 extra orders a week come from 11 tests spending $15 a day, priced off 2-order histories.
5. **The deal calendar isn't read.** Lightning Deals fall on 30 Sep, 7 Oct (Prime Big Deal Days), 12, 24 and 30 Oct. 19 predictions grade on 6 Oct and 29 on 7 Oct, both Prime Big Deal Days.
6. **Its cuts on ranking terms come from a class setting, not from pricing logic.** They cite the "Maintenance Profit Control" canvas, class HARD (operator 2026-09-24/25, "−25% off the top-of-search price"). So does the budget halving on "king size silk sheet set".
7. **Its focus list points S6 at S4's terms.** S6's focus is Satin, Satin Queen, Satin Full and Satin Twin, the groups where S4 ranks #3–12 and converts better. It excludes Silk and King, where S6 wins. So every ranking move is a cut, and S6's best syntax is tapered.

**Why sales fell.** S6 lost traffic, not conversion: sessions fell 73–80% from the spring and July peaks while click-through held at the market rate. Two events explain it:
- King Black was out of stock for 35 days (13 Aug–16 Sep). The King terms crashed and came back after the restock.
- Spend was cut from $296 to $87 a day from 27 Aug. The terms that lost paid clicks are the ones that lost rank.

The title cut and the September image changes are minor or not supported as causes.

## 5. Bids and placements on ranking campaigns

1. **Price on the effective top-of-search bid** (base × (1 + modifier)), never on CPC.
2. **A ranking term's top-of-search price never falls as a side effect of a base cut.**
3. **Where product pages take more than 20% of a ranking campaign's clicks,** cut the base by up to 25% a write and raise the modifier so the top-of-search price holds.
   - Product pages pay worst: 7.6% conversion and 31.5% ACoS (10–23 Sep, settled).
   - Never cut the base to cents to reach the top of search. Rest of search and product pages carry about half of the ranking campaigns' clicks, and rest of search pays best (10.0% conversion, 18.7% ACoS).
4. **Rest of search gets its own modifier once earned** (15+ clicks converting at or above the base rate); until then it stays at 0. Rows 94, 179 and 269 load.
5. **Pushes climb 20–30% a write,** capped by:
   - the engine's market bound (its top-of-search CPC + 15%);
   - the syntax group's clearing CPC (Satin $2.03, Satin Queen $2.50, Satin King $2.55).
6. **The daily check:**
   - If top-of-search clicks run under 70% of plan for 3 non-deal days with budget intact, raise the next write.
   - If the budget runs out, raise the budget instead.
   - Don't cut the top-of-search price until 70% of the planned clicks arrive.
7. **Rows above the engine's own market bound are held for Joseph:**
   - 45, 48, 49 and 50, the "queen size sheet set silk" campaign: the engine flags two keywords above its $2.89 bound, "queen sheets set silk" at $3.47 (row 48) and "queen size sheet set silk" at $3.40 (row 49). ## Appendix A: fixes for the engine

1. **Spend:** S6-attributed ad spend for the same dates as sales. Split shared campaigns by advertised-ASIN spend. List every campaign that advertises S6, including the S4 LTSF auto and the conquest campaign.
2. **Margin:** per-child Sellerboard margin, excluding one-offs, for ceilings; all-in for profit.
3. **Hero:** the size's best seller with 30 or more days of cover. A zero sales rate is UNKNOWN, never GREEN. Give King White its catalogue colour, and treat "Striped Red" as red.
4. **Product ads:** read and write the product-ad roster. Load back-up ads paused, with a 14-day switch rule. Never read the advertised child from the campaign name.
5. **Stock:** read stock on the audit day from Sellerboard and the shipment tracker, with its source and time. Re-point any campaign whose only in-stock child falls under 14 days of cover.
6. **Deals:** read the deal calendar. Hold climbs, revives and launches on deal days, and keep grade dates off them.
7. **Maintenance class, not only pricing code:**
   - exempt terms at target or inside the top 20, and the owned syntax;
   - cap base cuts at 25%;
   - no top-of-search fall as a side effect of a base cut;
   - never override "human-mandatory";
   - no cut under 15 clicks;
   - targets the run won't fund must not trigger cuts.
8. **Focus lists:** set per product to the groups it owns (S6 Silk King, S4 Satin and Queen). Apply the sibling-ownership check to revives as well as new campaigns.
9. **Confidence:** score keywords only on enough top-of-search clicks from the right placement. Use rank-band targets from the product's own data. Use one rank source with a 7-day median (the two daily tables differ by 7.6 places on average).
10. **Tests and harvest:** harvest into existing exacts at the source CPC with isolation negatives. Price test orders at clicks × a shrunk conversion rate, on their own forecast line. Export every target of a build.
11. **Negatives:** never wall a converting term; use a statistical zero-order rule.
12. **Budgets:** cut only budgets that bind; drop no-op rows.
13. **Governance:**
    - Carry the agent review’s holds forward.
    - Keep a 14-day observation lock and one lever per campaign per cycle.
    - Verify deployment before the next run.
    - Grade predictions.
    - Route every state change through a forecast line and the agent review.
14. **Non-hero guardrail:** heroes may run negative up to the ranking budget; non-hero SKUs combined must be at or above $0 net.
15. **Text:** fix the "Re-Launch" template, the "out of stock" wording on a red King with 11–12 units, date bugs, the percentage-versus-fraction mix and the kill date.
16. **Output:** one final row per campaign with the value to upload, after the review rules are applied, exported in the upload order.
