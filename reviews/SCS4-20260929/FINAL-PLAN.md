## Bottom line
- **Exact: keep the structure you have.**
  - Each size stays on its preferred, best-selling child wherever that child's stock is healthy.
  - Run at a moderate pace: no doses, no climbs this write, top-of-search price ≤ $3.22.
  - Reshape placements so Exact spend goes to top of search.
- **Support layer.** Broad, Phrase and Auto support the healthy slower colours. Dedicated campaigns only where the data shows the need:
  - one overstock Auto;
  - three colour-matched Exact campaigns (purple, pink, white-king);
  - a grey test once the Cool Grey 4PCS stock is live.
- **Queen:** stays on Queen Graphite.
- **King:** moves to King White, because King Graphite runs out around 6 Oct with nothing inbound.
- **King Cool Grey 4PCS:** a support SKU, not the King Exact owner.
- **Spend:** about $1,750–1,850 a week in total (now $1,741). TACoS ceiling 13%; September was 11.7%.

## Stock tiers used
- **Planning pace:** the higher of September's daily sales and the Apr–Sep in-stock rate.
- **A – healthy:** at least 90 days of cover at 1.5× planning pace. Can carry Exact; a moderate push is allowed.
- **B – steady:** at least 60 days on hand, or at least 90 including inbound. Maintain; no push.
- **C – protect:** below both thresholds. Cut bids; no push.
- **OUT:** 5 units or fewer.

## Why King White carries King, not King Cool Grey
- **King White on the data:**
  - #2 in-stock King seller in September (36 units), essentially tied with Light Blue (37) and Light Grey (35).
  - Best King conversion: 12.6% unit session.
  - The only King stock that can absorb Exact traffic: 769 units, about 640 days.
- **The others fail the stock test:**
  - Light Blue has 68 units and nothing inbound; it would go out of stock.
  - Light Grey and Cream are thin until their inbound lands.
- **King Cool Grey 4PCS:**
  - 216 units across five READY_TO_SHIP shipments, so around mid-Nov. That is about 99 days at its own historic in-stock pace (2.18/day) and about 66 days at 1.5×.
  - It is a new ASIN. The engine has no price or landed cost for it.
  - Use: King Broad, King Phrase, Auto and a grey colour test.
  - It moves to King head terms only if, after 4 weeks live, it converts at least as well as King White and still has 90+ days of cover.
- **Economics caveat.** King White earns $18.86 a unit, excluding a one-off $539 inbound fee ($3.87 strict), against King Graphite's $28.54. So its top-of-search ceiling is lower (~$2.52–2.62), and King Exact prices step down rather than climb.
