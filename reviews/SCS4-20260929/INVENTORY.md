## Bottom line
The same thing has happened three times in 12 months. Each time the family reached the top 5 organically, the colours that sell ran out 6–10 weeks later and the rank collapsed with them.

The cause is reorder timing and colour mix. Plans were created with 2–6 weeks of stock left, while it takes about 5–6 weeks from plan to sellable stock. PPC volume is not the main driver.

**Recommendation: a controlled, stock-gated push.**
- Don't sell out at break-even. Stock is 3,820 units plus 2,382 ready to ship (about 7.5 months at September's pace), and September's net margin was 17.7%.
- Don't push the whole family.
