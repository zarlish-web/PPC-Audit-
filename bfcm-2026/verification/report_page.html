Method and limits

  1. New code reads the original records directly and does not call the plan's scripts or the tracker's builder.
  2. Break-even = (price − COGS − FBA − referral − 1% deal fee − $70 per deal) ÷ price (Amazon's deal fees, corrected Nov 2 from 1.5% + $100). Cap = the lower of 80% of break-even and the growth-band ceiling. Plan TACoS = TACoS now × deal-period factor, raised to the band floor, never above the cap.
  3. Account figures were pulled fresh on Nov 2. Per-product SP spend was compared with the ads console as a second record.
  4. Business rules applied as documented: Satin 4 Pc Twin counted under Satin 6 Pc; a name list for re-parented 2025 listings; Liquidation products judged on units.
  5. Limits: the deal sheet, SKU lists and inventory export have no second record, so they were checked for internal consistency. Three no-deal prices come from the listing price only.
