## PPC push vs price
- **Sponsored Products spend on White was flat** ($707 → $708/day), so the SP push is not what moved White.
- **SB and video spend rose** after the new creative went live on Oct 17–19. It rose on the other colours too ($10 → $110/day), so it is not White-specific.
- Of the extra 2.9 White units/day, 0.7 came through ads and 2.2 were organic.
- **B4 Queen push (from Oct 28):** spend $2 → $47/day, units flat. Too early to read.
- **B6 Queen push:** spend $28 → $139/day for +1.7 units/day, with net profit down $51/day. Most of that gain is the push, not the price.
- **Week 2 (Oct 27 – Nov 1) carries a market-wide lift:** other colours' conversion also rose. That is why the uncut colours are used as the comparison.

## Read
- **B4: the cut is working, mainly on King White.**
  - Conversion doubled and units rose 49%, while B4's other colours fell 24%.
  - Net profit improved by $38/day.
  - Watch: B4 total units still fell 15%, so part of White's gain may be buyers switching from other colours.
- **B6: no price effect.**
  - White rose 11% against 8% for the colours that weren't cut. The $5 is margin given away.
  - The real B6 problem is King White's ads: about 90% TACoS and -$268/day, both before and after the cut.
- **Overall:**
  - The cut paid for itself before ads (+$84/day).
  - After ads, net profit is down $50/day because SB and video spend rose $117/day.
- **Caveats:**
  - Volumes are small: Queens sell 2–4 units/day.
  - The strike-through price showed on only 5 of 10 SKUs as of Oct 23.
  - A lower price in the last 30 days can lower the maximum deal price Amazon allows for BF/CM. Check this against the King White deal price.
