# Amazon US / CA / UK: Fees and Advertising Benchmarks for a Bedding Feasibility Study

Prepared 2026-10-26. Product scope: sheet sets, duvet cover sets, quilts, pillowcases, mattress protectors and toppers, made in Pakistan.

---

## 0. How this was researched (read first)

- **Access limits.** This environment's egress proxy blocked direct fetches of every web host, including sellercentral.amazon.*, sell.amazon.*, sellingpartners.aboutamazon.com, m.media-amazon.com (rate-card PDFs) and all secondary sites. All figures below come from **web-search result extracts**: the search engine's summaries and quotes of the pages listed. No page was opened in full.
- **What that means.** Where an official Amazon page or PDF appeared in the search results and its text was quoted, the row is marked **Official (via search extract)**. Where only third-party blogs agreed, it is marked **Secondary, consensus**. Where sources disagreed or only one source existed, it is marked **Low**.
- **Before final pricing.** Run each candidate SKU (real dimensions and weight) through the **FBA Revenue Calculator** in each marketplace, or check the Fee Preview report. This matters most for **Canada and UK FBA fees**, which I could not confirm from an Amazon rate card.
- **"As-of" dates.** Each date is the fee's effective date as reported, plus the date the source was checked or published where known. All search lookups were run on 2026-10-26.
