# Bedding Market Research: Amazon US / Canada / UK launch feasibility for a Pakistan-based manufacturer

Research date: 2026-10-26. Prepared as input to a feasibility study.

## 0. How to read this document (method and limitations)

- **Access limitation.** Direct page fetching was blocked in this research environment for almost every domain (Amazon, Marketplace Pulse, NYT/Wirecutter, Good Housekeeping, PBS-reporting Pakistani news sites, Wikipedia). All figures below were obtained from **web-search result extracts** of the cited pages. Each figure carries the source URL. Where the page's own publication date was visible it is given; otherwise the date is marked **"n.d."** (not displayed) or as a **snapshot** (for Amazon Best Seller pages, which change hourly and whose crawl dates were not shown, or were shown as "~N days old").
- **Labels used.**
  - **[Official]** = government statistics (e.g., Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, USTR, US Supreme Court).
  - **[Trade press]** = news / trade-journal reporting of data.
  - **[Estimate]** = third-party market-research estimate. Methodology is noted where the source gave it, which was rare.
  - **[Low-quality]** = report-mill, AI-generated, or SEO / affiliate source. Shown only for completeness; do not rely on it.
  - **[Indicative]** = B2B listing or vendor price guide. Not a quote.
  - **[Snapshot]** = Amazon Best Seller page as indexed by the search engine. Rank, price and rating counts are approximate and date-uncertain.
- **Conflicts.** Where sources conflict, the range is shown.
- **No numbers were invented.** Where no source was found, the cell says so.

### 2.3 UK specifics

**UK mattress sizes:**
- Single 90×190 cm
- Double 135×190 cm
- King 150×200 cm
- Super King 180×200 cm

**UK duvet / duvet-cover sizes:**
- Single 135×200 cm
- Double 200×200 cm
- King 225×220 cm (some guides give 230×220 cm)
- Super King 260×220 cm

Sources: https://www.scottsofstow.co.uk/page/duvet-sizes ; https://woodsfinelinens.com/blogs/journal/uk-bed-linen-sizes-a-clear-guide-to-a-perfect-fit (n.d.)

**UK bed-linen formats.** The UK is duvet-led. The core UK purchase is:
- a **duvet cover set** (cover + 1 or 2 pillowcases, e.g. 50×75 cm housewife pillowcases); plus
- a separately bought **fitted sheet**, typically with a "deep pocket" of 25–40 cm.

The US-style four-piece "flat + fitted + 2 pillowcases" sheet set is a smaller category on Amazon.co.uk. This is evidenced by the best-seller structure below:
- The Fitted Sheets and Duvet Cover Sets lists carry products with 100k+ ratings.
- Flat Sheets best sellers have only ~2–18k ratings.

No public survey quantifying UK flat-sheet usage was found. A YouGov GB survey (28 Jan 2026, n=7,019) asked about seasonal bedding changes, but the labels were not visible: https://yougov.co.uk/topics/politics/survey-results/daily/2026/01/28/c0a18/3

**UK seasonality evidence.** Dunelm attributes autumn / winter strength to "Winter Warm" ranges and 15-tog duvets, and says winter-sale timing moved quarterly bedding sales by ~4 points. Source: https://corporate.dunelm.com/media/ph0hfyo5/dunelm-fy23-q2-trading-update.pdf (Jan 2023).

## 7. Summary implications for the feasibility study (analyst synthesis)

1. **The proof-case exists.** Utopia Bedding, made by Pakistan's Utopia Industries, holds #1 positions across Amazon UK bedding and top ranks in US sheets, protectors and pillowcases. A vertically integrated Pakistani mill can build an Amazon brand. Its moat, however, is extreme low price (UK fitted sheet ~£6–9; US queen set USD 13–16) and enormous review counts (100k–210k).
2. **Do not enter on microfibre price.** China / Utopia hold that floor. Pakistan's structural advantage is **cotton** (percale / sateen, 200–400 TC).
3. **UK.**
   - Pakistan already supplies ~half or more of UK bed-linen imports. DCTS gives (or historically gave) duty-free access.
   - The **India-UK CETA (in force 15 Jul 2026) removes India's 8–12% UK duty handicap**, and Indian cotton brands (Pizuna-type) are now duty-level competitors.
   - UK assortment should be **fitted sheets + duvet cover sets + pillowcase pairs in UK sizes** (single / double / king / super king, 25–40 cm pockets), not US-style 4-piece sheet sets.
4. **US.**
   - India dominates cotton bed linen imports (~59% vs Pakistan ~19%).
   - After the IEEPA strike-down, Pakistan and India both face the 10% s301 add-on plus MFN. The tariff gap vs India is roughly neutral, while China faces higher stacked duties.
   - **Bangladesh's new TRQ** is a watch item.
5. **Cost headwinds.** Pakistan's energy cost is ~1.5–2.5× regional peers (APTMA figures), domestic cotton is short, and FY26 bed-wear exports were flat. Bed-wear exports in Q1 FY27 are flat too (-0.07%).
6. **Product differentiation that answers review pain points:**
   - long-staple single-ply yarn;
   - pre-shrunk fabric;
   - post-wash-verified deep pockets with all-round elastic;
   - side labels;
   - OEKO-TEX;
   - honest thread count.
