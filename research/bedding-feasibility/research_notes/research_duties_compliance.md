# Pakistan-made bed linen to Amazon FBA (US, Canada, UK): duties, tax, compliance and logistics

**Prepared:** 2026-10-26
**Scope:** Finished bed linen made in Pakistan (Karachi / Port Qasim) and shipped to Amazon FBA in the USA, Canada and the UK.

## How this was researched (read first)

- **How sources were read.** In this session the sandbox blocked direct page loads. Every official domain (usitc.gov, cbp.gov, federalregister.gov, cbsa-asfc.gc.ca, trade-tariff.service.gov.uk, gov.uk, ftc.gov and others) returned "CONNECT 403" or a DNS failure. All web evidence therefore came from search-engine extraction of the cited pages, run on **2026-10-26**. The URL listed is the page the search engine quoted. Where two sources disagreed, the table says so.
- **Search budget.** The shared web-search budget ran out partway through. Some areas could not be checked live:
  - most of part B (labelling)
  - all of part C (freight and 3PL pricing)
  - most of part D (Pakistan side)
  - the UK DCTS rules of origin for chapter 63
  - the Canadian rates for 9404.
- **What the status column means.**
  - **V-sec**: confirmed by two or more independent secondary sources that mirror the official text, or one secondary source that quotes the official document.
  - **V-off**: the official document was located and its content quoted through search.
  - **NV**: not verified this session. It is background knowledge from before mid-2026 and must be checked before you rely on it.
- **No rate has been made up.** Where no figure could be confirmed, the cell says "not confirmed".

Notes:

- **Man-made fibres.** Viscose or rayon made from bamboo is an artificial man-made fibre. It is classified as MMF (6302.22 or 6302.32), not as "other vegetable fibre" in 6302.39. (Section XI chapter note on "man-made fibres": NV.)
- **Blends.** A blend is classified by the fibre that predominates by weight (Section XI Subheading Note 2). A 60/40 cotton/polyester sheet goes in 6302.31; a 40/60 blend goes in 6302.32. (NV)
- **Sets.** A sheet set (flat sheet, fitted sheet and pillowcases) is usually reported line by line under each component's statistical suffix. All components of a plain cotton set share the same 8-digit rate. (NV)
- **Rates that might apply but are unconfirmed.** The Chapter 99 "9902" Miscellaneous Tariff Bill lines (e.g. 9902.17.45 for some cotton pillows) need checking. The MTB lapsed on 31 Dec 2020 and current status was not verified.

## 7. WORKED LANDED-DUTY BURDEN (illustrative)

Example: one ocean entry with goods worth **US$40,000 FOB Karachi**.

| Scenario | US (MFN + 10% Section 301 + MPF + HMF) | Canada (MFN; GST recoverable) | UK (DCTS 0% if origin met; VAT via postponed accounting) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(a) 100% cotton sheet set, plain or printed, not napped, no trim** (6302.31.90 / 6302.21.90) | 6.7% + 10% = **16.7% duty** ($6,680) + MPF $138.56 + HMF $50 = **$6,868.56, about 17.2% of FOB** | **17% duty** ($6,800). GST 5% on $46,800 = $2,340, recoverable if GST-registered. **Net about 17%** | **0% duty.** VAT 20% on CIF + duty is reclaimed through postponed VAT accounting. **Net about 0%.** If origin fails: 12% duty on CIF. |
| (a') same set but **with embroidery or piping trim** (6302.31.50) | 20.9% + 10% = **30.9%** + fees, about 31.4% | 17% | 0% (DCTS) or 12% |
| (a'') **cotton flannel**, napped, no trim (6302.31.70) | 3.8% + 10% = 13.8% + fees, about 14.3% | 17% | 0% or 12% |
| **(b) bamboo viscose or polyester microfibre set**, plain or printed, no trim (6302.32.20 / 6302.22.20) | 11.4% + 10% = **21.4% duty** ($8,560) + MPF $138.56 + HMF $50 = **$8,748.56, about 21.9%** | **17%** (6302.32 not individually confirmed) | **0%** (DCTS) or 12% |
| (b') MMF set with trim (6302.22.10; 6302.32.10 assumed the same) | 14.9% + 10% = 24.9% + fees | 17% | 0% or 12% |

How the bases differ:

- **US and Canada** charge duty on an FOB-type value.
- **UK** charges on CIF.
- **If the forced-labour Section 301 tariff is struck down**, the US burden drops to MFN plus fees: about 7.2% for (a) and about 11.9% for (b). Refunds would depend on the court's remedy and on entries not being final.

## 8. WHAT DEPENDS ON EXACT PRODUCT COMPOSITION AND CONSTRUCTION

1. **Fibre that predominates by weight** decides cotton (6302.21 / .31) versus man-made fibre (6302.22 / .32) versus other (6302.39). In the US the difference is 6.7% versus 11.4%. A 50/50 blend follows the heading that comes last in numerical order (Section XI note: NV). Viscose from bamboo is man-made, and "bamboo" fibre that is not viscose-processed is very rare.
2. **Printed versus not printed** decides 6302.21/.22 versus 6302.31/.32. The US rate is the same at 6.7% or 11.4%, but the statistical reporting differs.
3. **Decorative trim** (embroidery, lace, braid, edging, piping, appliqué, and possibly hemstitching) is the biggest trap in the US. Cotton goes from 6.7% to 20.9%; MMF goes from 11.4% to 14.9%. For quilts, piping over 6.35 mm moves 9404.40.10 (4.4%) to 9404.40.90 (12.8%). Avoid decorative piping or embroidery on US-bound SKUs, or get a CBP binding ruling.
4. **Napped** fabric (flannel or brushed) changes the US line. Cotton napped and untrimmed is 3.8%, against 6.7% for not napped.
5. **Filled or padded versus unfilled.** A comforter, quilt with batting, padded mattress protector or pillow goes to 9404 (US 4.4% to 12.8%; 7.3% for 9404.90.96; 5.3% or 6% for pillows; Canada 14% for 9404.40). An unpadded coverlet or bedspread goes to 6304. A plain fitted protector goes to 6302.
6. **Shell fibre** of comforters: a cotton shell is 4.4%; an MMF shell is 12.8% in the US.
7. **Laminates and non-wovens** (TPU or PVC-backed protectors, polypropylene non-woven) can change classification, and they trigger Prop 65 and PFAS checks.
8. **Fabric origin.** US origin for 6302 follows where the fabric was woven, not where it was sewn. UK DCTS origin needs Pakistani yarn or fabric processing (double transformation) or permitted cumulation. Sewing imported fabric can lose both "Made in Pakistan" status in the US and 0% in the UK.
9. **Children's bedding** triggers CPSIA (US), CCPSA (Canada) and GPSR (UK) requirements.
10. **Finishes** (stain or water repellent) can make the goods non-compliant in CA, ME, MN, CO and VT if they use PFAS.
