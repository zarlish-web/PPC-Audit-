# Placement modifiers

Set per campaign per placement, from that campaign's own conversion data. Never a blanket percentage in either direction — a blanket 0% is the same mistake as a blanket 135%, just cheaper.

## Read the full metric set first

CPA alone will mislead. Pull impressions, clicks, orders, CTR, CVR, CPC, CPA and revenue per click by placement, across all three windows.

Worked example:

| Placement | CTR | CVR | CPC | CPA | Rev/click | Share of orders |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Top of Search | 5.03% | 3.52% | $1.10 | $31.38 | $1.42 | 62% |
| Rest of Search | 2.03% | 2.60% | $0.49 | $19.02 | $1.11 | 21% |
| Product Pages | 0.31% | 2.15% | $0.47 | $22.06 | $0.82 | 17% |

Top of Search is the **best** placement on conversion and on revenue per click, and carries 62% of orders. It looks worst only on CPA, and that is entirely the CPC — 2.2× the others. A 35% conversion advantage does not cover a 124% price premium.

**So the fix is usually to reduce the modifier, not remove it.** At a 135% modifier a $0.53 base bid becomes $1.25; at +30% it runs near $0.69, which keeps the conversion advantage and brings CPA close to the other placements.

## Tiers

Judged per campaign per placement, on that campaign's own 30-day data:

| Condition | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Converting at or under $10 CPA | +30% |
| $10.01–$15 | +20% |
| $15.01–$20 | +10% |
| $20.01 to the ceiling | 0% — still serves at base bid |
| Above the ceiling | 0% |
| Clicks with no orders | 0% |
| No delivery in 30 days | 0%, re-read at day 14 |

Under a clearance objective, cap Top of Search lower than the other placements — it is the placement that drives rank, and rank is not the objective. Up to +30% where its own CPA sits inside the ceiling is reasonable; a blanket high premium is not.

## Expect few campaigns to earn a premium

On one product, 43 of 1,341 campaign-placement combinations qualified — 3.2%. The rest serving at base bid is the correct default, not a penalty.

## Hold everything at 0% when the window is contaminated

If the listing changed mid-window, or two windows disagree about which placement is better, set nothing and say why. Setting a premium on a contaminated read is worse than setting none.
