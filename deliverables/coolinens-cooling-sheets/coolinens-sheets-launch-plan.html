The plan in brief

  1. Lead with Queen. Eight Queen keywords get a full ranking push to top 5 by week 8 (top 20 by week 2, top 10 by week 4), with the top of search bid at each keyword's ranking ceiling.
  2. Every other ranking keyword targets top 10. The head terms rank through Queen sales velocity; King, Full, Twin and Cal King run as capped probes until the size is assigned to CooLinens.
  3. Bids come from the market: Amazon's suggested median for this listing on every keyword, capped at our ranking ceiling. Sleephoria and Decolure are reference only.
  4. Batch 1 plans about 69 clicks, 5.5 ad orders and $439 ad sales a day for $171 of spend.
  5. Batch 2 (40 keywords plus the other ad types) opens from 29 October when the Batch 1 gates pass. Batch 3 (6 keywords) runs in February to March with the re-order.

How bids are set

Base bidAmazon's suggested median for this listing on the keyword (paused bid check campaign, 10 Oct), capped at the ranking ceiling.
Ranking ceilingPrice x ranking ceiling ACoS (1.5 x break-even, Queen 56.4%) x expected conversion: the most a click can cost and still pay for rank.
Queen pushesTop of search modifier in 25 point steps, up to +200%, so the top of search bid lands on the ceiling. Amazon's suggested high understates top of search cost on head terms.
Clicks neededSearches a day x market clicks per search x the click share a target rank takes (top 5: 4.3%, top 10: 2.8%) x the 66% paid share during a push.

Verification

Every input checked against its source on 10 October 2026. Full detail on the Verification tab of the workbook.

  • Costs. COGS taken from SellerBoard for every size (Queen $25.90, King $30.02, Cal King $31.62, Full $24.61, Twin $19.94). FBA uses the Amazon fee preview of 8 Oct, which runs $0.26 to $0.55 above the 28 Sep pricing sheet: the conservative choice.
  • Search volume. The seasonal estimate ran 5% to 15% above the current market on the main terms (ASINsight, 6 Oct). Every keyword with a current read now uses it.
  • Click share by rank. Top 5 = 4.3% and top 10 = 2.8% of a page's clicks. Consistent with the sister listing at #3 (6.7%) and #4 (4.9%), and with the top 3 positions taking a median 44% of clicks on the Batch 1 pages.
  • Conversion. The sister listing converted 10.5% on our Batch 1 terms at $71 to $78 (3,010 clicks); x 0.8 = 8.4%, against a plan average of 7.9%. Nine keywords with 100+ clicks now use their price-matched rate; cooling sheets queen moves from 9.5% to 7.7%.
  • Target ranks. Raised: Queen pushes to top 5, every other ranking keyword to top 10. Each full push is funded to 100% of what its target needs.
  • Ceilings and bids. Every ceiling recomputed outside the workbook (price x ceiling ACoS x CVR) matches; every base bid and top of search bid sits at or under its ceiling.
  • Budgets. Every campaign budget is at least its planned spend x 1.25, minimum $10.
  • Upload file. Generated from the workbook and checked line by line: 64 of 64 bids, modifiers and budgets match; the Amazon 21 point gate passes.

Weekly rule for every ranking keyword, from week 2

  • Scale: CVR at plan, rank up 25% week on week, ACoS within the ceiling. Keep the bid; raise the budget if it capped out.
  • Hold: rank improving under 25% with CVR at plan. Check top of search share.
  • Reduce: ACoS over the ceiling for 2 weeks, or CVR under 60% of plan on 50 clicks. Modifier down one step, then the base 15% to 25%.
  • Taper: target held 2 weeks. Bids down 15% to 25% per cycle toward the profitable CPC.
  • Stock: advertised child under 21 days of cover. Switch to the backup child the same day.
  • Escalate: a verdict that repeats 4 cycles without its predicted effect is a diagnosis error.
