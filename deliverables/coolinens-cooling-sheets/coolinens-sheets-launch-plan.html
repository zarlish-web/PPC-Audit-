The plan in five lines

  1. Lead with Queen. Eight Queen keywords get a full ranking push with the top of search bid taken to each keyword's ranking ceiling; head terms and the King, Full, Twin and Cal King sizes enter capped while Sleephoria holds them.
  2. Bids come from the market: Amazon's suggested median for this listing on every keyword (bid check, 10 Oct), capped at our ranking ceiling. Sleephoria and Decolure are reference only.
  3. Exact match and fixed bids on day 1, plus product targeting on 12 premium rival pages and a sheets brand campaign. No auto on day 1.
  4. Planned spend about $136 a day for about 4.8 ad orders and $384 ad sales a day; budgets total $431.
  5. Batch 2 (40 keywords) and the other ad types open from 29 October only when the Batch 1 gates pass; Batch 3 in February to March with the re-order.

How bids are set

Base bidAmazon's suggested median for this listing on the keyword, from a paused bid check campaign on 10 Oct. Capped at the ranking ceiling.
Ranking ceilingPrice times the ranking ceiling ACoS (1.5 times break-even, Queen 55.9%) times expected conversion. The most a click can cost and still pay for rank.
Queen pushesTop of search modifier in 25 point steps, up to +200%, taking the top of search bid to the ceiling. Amazon's suggested high understates top of search cost on the head terms (cooling sheets queen: $1.44 suggested, $2.16 paid by the sibling).
Everything elseBase at the median, no modifier. Capped probes read conversion; they do not chase top of search. Product targets: median capped at break-even per click at 8% conversion.

Weekly rule for every ranking keyword, from week 2

  • Scale: CVR at or above plan, rank up 25% week on week, ACoS within the ceiling. Keep the bid; raise the budget if it capped out.
  • Hold: rank improving under 25% with CVR at plan. Check top of search share.
  • Reduce: ACoS over the ceiling for 2 weeks, or CVR under 60% of plan on 50 clicks. Modifier down one step, then the base 15% to 25%.
  • Taper: target rank held 2 weeks. Bids down 15% to 25% per cycle toward the profitable CPC.
  • Stock: advertised child under 21 days of cover. Switch to the backup child the same day.
  • Escalate: any verdict that repeats 4 cycles without its predicted effect is a diagnosis error.

Risks

  • Sibling auction: Sleephoria still pays more than our ceiling on cooling queen sheets ($4.46) and queen bed sheets cooling ($3.87). Without D1 those two pushes cannot win top of search.

  • Price parity: same knit, colours and price as Sleephoria ($78.39 in October). The listing has to carry the premium.
  • Stock: 4,024 units and nothing inbound; Queen Graphite 373 units carries every Queen push (switch to Cool Grey at 21 days of cover).
  • Season: searches run at a quarter of the summer level, so rank is cheap but slow; the return comes in spring.
